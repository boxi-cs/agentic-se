Agentic Software Engineering · Week 6

Review before you ask

Review and delegate a bounded change

6:07 · subtitles: English, 中文 · download

The idea

In a coding workflow, the person reviewing a change writes their own review first, names a concrete input and reproduces the failure. Only then do they ask for an AI review, whose findings are hypotheses to check against the contract. A revision is accepted on the unchanged checks, not on how clear it looks, and the branch that is merged is the one that must be tested.

This week

Lecture
Compare a specification, human findings and agent findings; decide ownership and integration checks.
Workshop
P2: review one short change against its acceptance criteria before reading any supplied or agent review, reproduce a finding or support a no-finding with the inputs checked, and revise after feedback.
Deliverables
P2 specification-and-review evidence
