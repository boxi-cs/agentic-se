Review before you ask

5:51 · subtitles: English, 中文 · download

Write your own review of a change first. Then treat each finding in a model’s review as a hypothesis: mark it confirmed, refuted or cannot tell, with the evidence, and for “cannot tell” name the smallest next check. A review that reports nothing says only that no finding was reported: it does not clear the change, and the person who merges it is responsible for accepting it.
