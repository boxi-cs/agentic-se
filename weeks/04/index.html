Film

A request or a gate

7:15 · subtitles: English, 中文 · download

Leave a rule as a written request only if it is acceptable for the agent to skip it. Otherwise enforce it with a hook you can test, or a human review step, and keep one record of it blocking and one of it passing. The film shows a hook stopping an unused import before a commit. It then treats a subagent the same way: its role is a contract, and what it hands back is a proposal until you open what it cites.
