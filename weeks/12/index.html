Film

Has it ever failed?

9:53 · subtitles: English, 中文 · download

Take the person out of a pipeline stage only after you have seen its check fail on a bad change and its stop path work. “The pipeline runs itself” is a claim about every change: one wrong result refutes it, and a good run proves only that run. In the film the gate stops the first proposal, and a second proposal passes every automatic stage while breaking the contract. A green gate supports only a smaller claim: the proposal agrees with the contract on these nine cases.
