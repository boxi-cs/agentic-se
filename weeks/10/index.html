Before you trust 75%

9:43 · subtitles: English, 中文 · download

A pass rate is a claim built from checks, records and a count. Before you trust one, ask three questions: can the check fail, was each pass earned, and does the count include every attempt? Report only what the audited records support, as a range where attempts are unresolved, and decide again when the scorer, the table or the rubric changes.
