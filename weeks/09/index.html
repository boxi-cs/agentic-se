Before you trust 25 points

Compare workflows with a fixed experiment

7:06 · subtitles: English, 中文 · download

The idea

A pass rate is a claim about checks, passes and a denominator. Before trusting one, ask three questions: can this check fail, was this pass earned, is the denominator intact? Then report the full declared rate beside a reliable subset chosen by check validity, and claim no more than the experiment observed.
